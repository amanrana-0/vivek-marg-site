Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

We collect only what we need to run your account and the programme. We never sell your information or use it for advertising. You can ask us to delete your account at any time by writing to vivekmarg@rkmviva.org.

Who we are

Vivek Marg (vivekmarg.org) is run by Ramakrishna Mission Vivekananda Institute of Values (VIVA), Gurugram, a unit of the Ramakrishna Mission. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean VIVA.

What we collect

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Your name, email address and phone number.
  • Whether you are a student, professor or institution, your college or university, and your year of study if you are a student.
  • Anything you choose to write in "Why do you want to join?", and whether you agreed to hear from us.
  • A password, if you sign up with email. We store it only in a scrambled (hashed) form that cannot be read back, not even by us.

When you use the site, we also save your progress in the course and the 21-Day Challenge, and the reflections you write, so you can pick up where you left off.

If you sign in with Google

If you choose "Continue with Google", Google shares your name, email address and a Google account ID with us. We use these only to create your account and sign you in. We do not get access to your Gmail, contacts, Drive or any other Google data, and we never see your Google password.

How we use your information

  • To create your account, sign you in and save your progress.
  • To send emails you ask for, such as a code to reset your password.
  • If you agreed when signing up, to contact you about Self-Development Pathway sessions and pre-reads.
  • To understand, in aggregate, how the programme is reaching students and institutions.

Ask Swamiji

Questions you type into Ask Swamiji are sent to Anthropic's Claude AI to generate an answer from the Complete Works of Swami Vivekananda. Please don't include personal details in your questions.

Who we share it with

We do not sell, rent or trade your information. We use a few service providers to run the site, and they handle data only on our behalf: Netlify (website hosting), Supabase (our database), Resend (sending emails), Anthropic (answering Ask Swamiji questions) and Google (if you choose Google sign-in). We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Cookies and tracking

We don't use advertising or analytics trackers. Your browser stores a sign-in token so you stay logged in; logging out removes it.

How long we keep it

We keep your account information for as long as your account exists. If you ask us to delete your account, we remove your personal information and progress from our database.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you, by emailing vivekmarg@rkmviva.org. If you signed in with Google, you can also remove Vivek Marg's access at any time from your Google Account settings, under "Third-party apps and services".

Children

Vivek Marg is designed for college students, faculty and institutions. It is not meant for children under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the date at the top.
